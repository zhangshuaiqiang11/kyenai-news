# Article editorial tiers and accountability

KyenAI assigns every published article an editorial tier so dated updates do not silently become evergreen advice.
The tier is a publication and review policy, not a quality score or a promise of search ranking.

## Tier A — durable decision asset

Tier A is reserved for source-backed pages that help readers make a durable security, governance, or procurement decision.
The visible answer, evidence boundary, and cited sources must be complete before publication. Review the page before every material change and at least every 14 days while the underlying product policy can change.

Tier A pages may receive supporting guides and internal links only when the new page contributes distinct evidence or a reproducible check.

## Tier B — dated source-backed update

Tier B covers time-bound product, company, and ecosystem updates. The article must anchor confirmed facts to a dated primary or official source and label interpretation or open questions separately. Review it when the source changes, a correction is needed, or a material announcement supersedes the record; otherwise check active topics at least every 30 days.

Tier B pages support durable guides, but a dated update must not be expanded into evergreen advice without new evidence.

## Tier C — supporting or experimental coverage

Tier C covers generic release, changelog, trend, or fallback coverage that does not yet earn a durable decision page. Keep the evidence boundary explicit and do not imply broad adoption, hands-on testing, or a ranking outcome. No expansion or repeated refresh is allowed without unique evidence, a clear reader task, and accountable editorial review.
